# Change log

## 0.1.0

- First release. Adds VAT (standard 18%, not-recoverable standard, zero-rated, exempt) and SSCL (2.5% with four activity portions: services 100%, wholesale and retail 50%, manufacture 85%, registered distributor 25%).
- Law cited: VAT Act No. 14 of 2002 as amended (Act No. 16 of 2024; IRD notice PN/VAT/2024-01, 2024-03-26; IRD notice SEC/PN/VAT/2026-03, 2026-07-03); SSCL Act No. 25 of 2022 as amended (Act No. 10 of 2026; IRD notice PN/SSCL/2026-04/1, 2026-04-16).
- Effective 2024-01-01 for rates; SSCL registration threshold for 2026-07-01.
- Before: none. After: the rules above.
